Our Story

A Space for
Creative Souls

Espazio is more than a destination — it's a cultural haven in the heart of Tacloban. A space for everyone, born from a passion for the arts, a love for local flavors, and a deep respect for Waray heritage. Winner of Tripadvisor Travellers' Choice Awards 2024 & 2025 and named Best Cafe in Tacloban City by Restaurant Guru 2025.

What We Offer

Three Pillars of Espazio

Each visit is a journey through the creative soul, the culinary heart, and the storied past of Tacloban.

01

Arts

Rotating exhibits from local and national artists, live performances, workshops, and a creative residency program that nurtures Waray talent.

02

Food

A curated menu that honors traditional Leyteño recipes while embracing modern culinary craft. Every dish tells a story of place and people.

03

History

Interactive heritage displays, curated archives, and storytelling sessions that bring the rich and resilient history of Eastern Visayas to life.
